




Tournament Winner
Problem Statement:

You are organizing a coding competition where several teams are participating. Each match is a head-to-head competition between two teams. At the end of each match, one team is declared the winner and the other team is the loser. The winner of each match earns 3 points. The team with the highest total points at the end of all matches is declared the overall winner of the tournament.

Write a function TournamentWinner that takes in two parameters:

competitions: A list of pairs representing the teams that have competed against each other.
results: A list of integers where each integer represents the result of the corresponding competition in the competitions list. A 1 in the results list means the home team (the first team in the competition pair) won, and a 0 means the away team (the second team in the competition pair) won.
